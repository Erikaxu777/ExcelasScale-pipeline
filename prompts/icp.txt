Develop an ICP and prospect-search plan for Excelas Scale.

The plan will guide a subsequent workflow that finds five named
professionals in the UK data-centre industry and can later expand
to fifty prospects.

PRODUCT CONTEXT

Excelas Scale helps technical teams record and review equipment
installation and site work using video, audio and sensor data.

AI assists the review process, while humans make the final decisions.

Ground proposed use cases in these capabilities. Describe potential
problems and benefits as hypotheses requiring validation.

TARGET COMPANIES

Focus on organisations serving UK data-centre projects, including:
- Data-centre operators.
- Installation and construction contractors.
- Electrical and structured-cabling contractors.
- Commissioning and technical-assurance firms.

Prioritise organisations whose work involves installation inspection,
testing, defect resolution, acceptance or operational handover.

FOUR SEARCH DIRECTIONS

Use the four role families specified in the task.

1. Installation QA/QC

People who define inspection requirements, manage installation quality,
review defects or assess evidence before acceptance.

Relevant responsibilities may include quality assurance processes,
inspection plans, field inspections and corrective-work verification.

2. Electrical

People who execute, supervise or evaluate electrical installation,
testing and verification.

Relevant responsibilities may include installation inspection,
commissioning evidence, readiness reviews and electrical acceptance.

3. Cabling

People who execute, supervise or evaluate structured cabling and
fibre installation.

Relevant responsibilities may include routing, labelling, terminations,
test records, as-built documentation and handover.

4. Operations

People responsible for live-site work, operational acceptance,
maintenance or the use of installation and handover records.

Relevant responsibilities may include reviewing site changes,
accepting completed work and resolving operational handback questions.

RESPONSIBILITY MATCHING

Match people using documented responsibilities.

Consider potential hands-on users, technical evaluators and internal
sponsors. Treat purchasing authority as a separate qualification question.

A person may match several search directions. Commissioning can overlap
with electrical work, quality review and operations.

Combine candidates across searches and deduplicate them. Select five
people from five different companies, with at most one person per
company. Prioritise responsibility fit and evidence quality within each
company, allowing overlap between role families.

UK RELEVANCE

Give highest priority to evidence that the individual manages UK
projects or has an explicit UK delivery or operations remit.

EMEA or global roles at companies with UK data-centre projects may
remain relevant. Flag the individual's UK project responsibility
as requiring confirmation when evidence is only at company level.

Distinguish UK location, company project coverage and personal
project responsibility when evaluating sources.

CONTACTABILITY

Prioritise publicly published work emails as the primary contact route.
Collect a matched personal LinkedIn profile as a supplementary channel.
Keep unavailable contact details unknown.

SEARCH PLAN

For each role family, provide:
- Three to five relevant responsibilities.
- Two or three testable pain hypotheses.
- Three to five realistic job titles, including common variants.
- Three or four English search queries.

Each pain hypothesis should connect a responsibility to a specific
installation-evidence or human-review problem worth investigating.

Queries should combine role or responsibility terms with data-centre
terms and UK scope. Include queries likely to surface employer
biographies, team pages and named project references.
